04 — Takeaway
Recommendation Memo

McLaren and Ferrari lead the grid on risk-adjusted, revenue-normalized performance — not just on raw championship points. Both convert revenue into results efficiently while running healthy operating margins.

Scale isn't the same as efficiency. Mercedes has the largest revenue base ($799M) and strongest operating profit ($202M) but finished 4th in points, so its per-dollar efficiency score gets pulled down even though it's the field's most financially stable team.

Risk flags: Aston Martin, Alpine, Williams and Sauber posted 2024 operating losses. Their financial-stability scores drag down their composite ranking — a team burning cash to compete is a riskier proposition for sponsors and investors than one that isn't.

Bottom line: for value-for-money, McLaren and Ferrari currently offer the best blend of competitive return and balance-sheet health. Mercedes is the lowest-risk single asset in the sport on financial stability alone; Haas is the standout small-budget efficiency play.
